Featured post - Preventive care

How to prepare for your annual wellness visit

An annual visit is more useful when it is focused. Bring a short list of current medications, recent symptoms, family history updates, and the questions you keep meaning to ask.

Before the appointment, check whether you are due for vaccines, screening labs, blood pressure review, cervical cancer screening, colon cancer screening, or bone health assessment. Your doctor can help prioritize what matters for your age, risks, and goals.

The best wellness visits end with a plan: what to monitor, what to change, which tests are due, and when follow-up should happen.
